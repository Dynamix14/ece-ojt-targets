The short answer: the six Makati CBD sites are a 6-minute drive from Taft, and three of them are genuinely electronics work — Meinhardt (ELV design), USHER (seismic instrumentation) and Edgepoint (tower RF). The silicon sits far away: Lattice in Alabang, TI in Baguio.

  • The Makati internships are light. APMC interns report “not much to do most of the time”; Meinhardt’s only review calls it a “light working environment”.
  • Banking pays. Security Bank interns confirm a ₱10k+ allowance — and one cohort was paid more than two months after finishing.
  • Semiconductor gives real projects. TI and Lattice reviews both stress meaningful work plus a mentor; TI’s catch is a supervisor too busy to guide you.
  • The small firms are the wildcard. USHER’s single intern review is a 2.0 warning about zero training. No reviews exist at all for Jason Marine, 90 Degrees, PISAS, Secuna or Edgepoint.

  • Distance is road distance from DLSU–Taft (14.5649, 120.9931), generated by OSRM. Traffic-free: add 40–100% at rush hour.
  • Roles are inferred from each company’s published services — not from a vacancy. Only a green ✓ posting is a requisition I actually opened.

  • Ranks 1–2 and 3–5 sit inside the same Makati CBD blocks; treat sub-kilometre gaps as ties.
